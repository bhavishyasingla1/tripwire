      What to do now

      How Tripwire splits the work

      1. On device

        Patterns, instantly

        A scam-pattern list, updated weekly through Firebase Remote Config, gives a first answer in milliseconds.

      2. On device

        Gemini Nano reads it

        Through Firebase AI Logic in PREFER_ON_DEVICE mode, Nano finds warning signs, quotes the exact words and spots names. Every quote is checked against the text; invented ones are dropped.

      3. On device

        Personal details blanked

        Names, phone numbers, UPI IDs, Aadhaar, PAN, bank and card numbers become tokens like [PERSON_1]. The token map never leaves your browser.

      4. Cloud

        Company checked in the cloud

        Only the company name, domains and flagged, blanked lines go to cloud Gemini, with Google Search grounding where the project allows it. Real names are put back on your device.

      5. Cloud

        Community, as hashes

        Scam reports are stored in Firestore as SHA-256 hashes of the sender's details, never the details themselves.

      No Gemini Nano on this computer? Tripwire still works: it blanks personal details first and only then asks cloud Gemini. Screenshots go to the cloud only if you say yes.
